What ShipSafe checks

♿ Accessibility (ADA / WCAG)

Missing alt text, unlabeled form fields, no page language, empty buttons/links, heading structure — the exact issues cited in ADA demand letters.

🔒 Privacy & legal

Missing privacy policy, no cookie/consent notice, no terms or contact path — frequently bundled into the same complaints (CCPA/GDPR).

🔎 Schema & SEO

Broken or missing structured data, meta description, Open Graph, canonical — quietly costs you search visibility and rich results.

🛡️ Trust signals

HTTPS, favicon, mobile viewport, mixed-content — the basics that make a site look legitimate to visitors and crawlers.

FAQ

Is my AI-generated (Lovable / Bolt / v0 / Cursor) site really at risk?

Often, yes. AI builders produce great-looking sites that frequently ship with no lang attribute, unlabeled inputs, images without alt text, and no privacy policy. Those are precisely the issues that serial ADA filers scan for. ShipSafe flags them in plain English so you can fix them before a letter arrives.

Is this legal advice?

No. ShipSafe is an automated heuristic check that highlights common risk areas. It is not legal advice and not a guarantee of compliance. For a binding audit, talk to a professional.

How does it work without signup?

It fetches your page and analyzes the HTML right in your browser. Nothing is stored. The free check reads your page source; a deeper rendered-page scan and scheduled monitoring are coming in the Pro tier.

Why might a check say it couldn't read my site?

Some sites block third-party fetching, or render entirely with JavaScript (so the HTML source is nearly empty). That's a limitation of the free in-browser check — the upcoming Pro scan renders the full page.
