Questions

Is my AI-generated site (Lovable, Bolt, v0, Cursor) really at risk?

Frequently, yes. These tools produce great-looking sites that often ship without a lang attribute, with unlabeled inputs and images missing alt text, and no privacy policy — precisely what automated ADA filers look for. ShipSafe surfaces those in plain English so you can fix them on your terms, not under a deadline.

Is this legal advice?

No. ShipSafe is an automated heuristic check that flags common risk areas. It isn't legal advice and isn't a guarantee of compliance. For a binding determination, talk to a qualified professional. See exactly what we check and how →

How does it work without a signup?

It fetches your page and analyzes the HTML in your browser — nothing is stored or sent to us. The free check reads your page's source; a deeper scan that renders the full page (catching contrast and JavaScript-built content) is in development.

It said it couldn't read my site. Why?

Some sites block third-party fetching, and some render entirely with JavaScript, leaving the HTML source nearly empty. The free in-browser check can only read static source — the upcoming rendered scan handles both.
