About ShipSafe

A free, no-signup check for the accessibility and privacy gaps that get websites demand letters — built for the wave of AI-generated and no-code sites.

Why it exists

AI builders and no-code tools made it possible for anyone to ship a beautiful site in an afternoon. But "beautiful" and "compliant" are different things. These tools routinely produce pages with no language attribute, unlabeled form fields, images without alt text, and no privacy policy — exactly the issues that automated ADA filers scan for, and that resulted in 4,600+ website lawsuits in 2024 alone.

Most existing checkers are built for developers and speak in error codes. ShipSafe is for the person who shipped the site, in plain language: here's your risk, here's what to fix, here's why it matters.

Your privacy

The check runs in your browser. Your URL is fetched and analyzed client-side; we don't store it, log it, or send it to a server of ours. There's no account and no tracking pixel waiting to flag your site.

Who's behind it

ShipSafe is a product of Copper Bay Labs, the product studio of Copper Bay Tech — a Sonoma County web, IT, and security shop that builds and audits sites for small businesses. ShipSafe is the free, self-serve front door to work we also do by hand.

Need more than a smoke test? Copper Bay Tech does full accessibility and compliance audits, remediation, and ongoing monitoring. Talk to us →

The fine print

ShipSafe gives automated, heuristic guidance. It is not legal advice, not a certified WCAG audit, and not a guarantee of compliance. For a binding determination, consult a qualified professional. See the methodology for exactly what we check and the limits of an in-browser scan.
